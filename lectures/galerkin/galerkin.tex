\chapter{Galerkin's Method}

The idea behind Finite-Difference Method is to look at PDEs as relations between derivatives, and convert them into Difference Equations that can be solved. The aim of Galerkin-type methods is to see PDEs as equations in infinite-dimensional spaces, and move them to finite-dimensional settings where they can be solved.
